Good to know

No flashing light, on purpose: light flickering at 3 to 30 times a second can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. Sound and vibration only.

The research on brainwave entrainment is mixed. Pulsed sound has some modest findings, mostly for relaxation; vibration has very little. Treat this as an experiment, and stop if anything feels unpleasant.

The vibration motor blurs beats together above about 10 a second, and becomes a steady buzz above about 15. Faster bands work as sound only.
