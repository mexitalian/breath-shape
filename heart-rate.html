Heart rate & HRV

How to measure
  1. Cover the back camera and the flash with one fingertip.
  2. Rest your hand on something and press lightly, without squashing.
  3. Keep still. Heart rate takes about 30 seconds; HRV needs a steady minute.

Tap the circle, then allow the camera when asked.

HRV is the beat-to-beat variation of your heart (RMSSD). Higher usually means more relaxed; it differs a lot between people, so compare your own readings, before and after a session.
